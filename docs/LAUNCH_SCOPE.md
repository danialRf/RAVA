# Practical Launch Scope

The architecture is broad; launch must remain achievable.

## Launch A — beautiful manual commerce

Can go live before scraping:
- storefront,
- admin,
- manual product entry,
- estimated price,
- quote,
- deposit,
- order tracking,
- product requests,
- card-to-card or fake gateway until merchant ready.

## Launch B — assisted automation

Add:
- 1–3 retailer adapters,
- deal inbox,
- pricing engine,
- deterministic product cards,
- Telegram draft/publish.

## Launch C — scaled automation

Add:
- more retailer adapters,
- seller trust,
- advanced search,
- alerts,
- automation policies.

## Launch D — optional AI

Only after revenue justifies it:
- richer product copy,
- campaign content,
- agent-assisted merchandising.

AI is not a prerequisite for revenue.
