# Phase 7 — Final launch MVP

This is the final pre-launch phase. It replaces the former phases 7–14 for the
initial release.

Required scope:

- customer decision after sourcing failure,
- balance collection,
- Germany departure and Iran-arrival propagation,
- local delivery and customer-visible tracking,
- cancellation/refund recording,
- production security/environment checks,
- deployment and recovery documentation,
- full automated verification.

Deferred until real usage justifies them:

- automated retailer ingestion and deal intelligence,
- marketing-card automation and Telegram publication,
- advanced search/personalization,
- CRM, reviews, promotions, referrals and loyalty.

The phase is complete only when `docs/PROJECT_STATE.md` and
`docs/PRODUCTION_CHECKLIST.md` state the remaining external launch blockers
without claiming the site is live.
