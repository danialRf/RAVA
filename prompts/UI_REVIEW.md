# RAVA UI Review Prompt

Act as an experienced Iranian ecommerce design director and frontend quality reviewer.

Inspect the implemented pages at mobile and desktop widths.

Reject generic AI-generated design.

Evaluate:

- Does the first mobile viewport help shopping immediately?
- Is Persian typography natural and legible?
- Does mixed Persian/Latin content render correctly?
- Does the brand feel warm, trustworthy and modern rather than ultra-luxury?
- Is product photography dominant enough?
- Are colors restrained?
- Are there unnecessary cards/pills/gradients/shadows?
- Is information hierarchy clear?
- Are source, estimate, deposit and delivery communicated near purchase action?
- Are filters/search usable with one hand?
- Are touch targets adequate?
- Are skeleton/empty/error states branded?
- Does the UI feel copied from a component-library demo?
- Does any decoration exist only because “AI websites do this”?

Take screenshots at:
360x800, 390x844, 430x932, 768px tablet, 1440px desktop.

Create a concise issue list and then fix all high-impact issues.
Re-run visual checks.
Do not redesign merely for novelty.
